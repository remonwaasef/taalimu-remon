# Data Breach Response Plan (GDPR Compliant)

**Last Updated:** {{ date('Y-m-d') }}
**Data Protection Officer (DPO):** dpo@taalimu.com

## 1. Introduction
This document outlines the standard operating procedure (SOP) for responding to data breaches at Taalimu, in accordance with Article 33 of the GDPR.

## 2. Definition of a Data Breach
A breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data.

## 3. Immediate Actions (Within 24 Hours)
1. **Containment:** The IT/Security team must immediately isolate affected systems to prevent further data loss.
2. **Assessment:** The DPO must assess the scope and risk of the breach (What data? How many users?).
3. **Internal Notification:** Notify the executive board and legal counsel.

## 4. Notification to Supervisory Authority (Within 72 Hours)
If the breach poses a risk to user rights and freedoms, the DPO must notify the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL) within 72 hours via their official portal.

**Required Information:**
- Nature of the breach.
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects.
- Contact details of the DPO.
- Likely consequences of the breach.
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

## 5. Notification to Data Subjects (Users/Students)
If the breach is likely to result in a **high risk** to the rights and freedoms of individuals (e.g., passwords or financial data exposed), we must communicate the breach to them without undue delay.

**Communication Channel:**
- Email alert to all affected users.
- In-app notification for active tenants.

## 6. Post-Breach Review
Within 14 days of containment, a post-incident review must be conducted to update security policies and prevent recurrence. All breaches must be recorded in the Internal Breach Register.
